FAQ

Common questions

What is the difference between an Online UPS and a Line Interactive UPS?

An Online UPS continuously supplies clean power from its inverter, which suits servers, labs and critical loads. A Line Interactive UPS regulates voltage and switches to battery on failure, which suits computers and small offices at lower cost.

What capacities do you supply?

Online UPS from 5 KVA up to 200 KVA, isolation transformers and CVT up to 25 KVA, and we have installed stabilizers up to 1000 KVA. Tell us your load and we'll size it.

Why choose lithium over a normal battery?

Our Lithium Ferro Phosphate range needs no maintenance and has a small footprint, which helps where space is limited.

Do you provide service and AMC?

Yes. We provide on-site consultation, installation and maintenance across India, with Annual Maintenance Contracts for all our products.
